# SILO Sales Kit

## Positioning
SILO is an intelligent business workspace that brings tasks, customer records, documents, calendar events, email drafts, repeatable workflows and business analytics together. Its AI Assistant helps owners understand their workspace. Its Business Memory keeps important company context in the workspace, and its specialist AI Employee role profiles can prepare proposed changes for an owner or administrator to review before they are applied.

Do not describe the current product as an autonomous AI workforce, a full accounting platform, a CRM replacement, or an AI system that independently runs a business. Those are future product directions, not capabilities in this release.

## Best-fit first customers
Start with owner-operated and small-to-growing businesses that currently coordinate work across scattered notes, spreadsheets, inboxes and task apps. Initial outreach verticals: hospitality/restaurants, retail/e-commerce, professional services, real estate and technology. SILO can be evaluated by other industries too; do not claim industry-specific compliance or workflows that have not been validated.

## Pricing
- Starter: $19 per business workspace per month.
- Pro: $49 per business workspace per month.
- Business: custom quote based on implementation and support scope.
- No per-employee billing.

Paid checkout is only live after the operator has configured and tested Stripe products, price IDs, webhook signing secret, customer portal and cancellation/payment-failure behavior. Do not promise a free trial until trial behavior and customer disclosures have been implemented and tested.

## 30-second pitch
SILO gives your business one organized place for the work that keeps operations moving: tasks, customers, documents, calendars, customer email drafts and repeatable workflows. Its AI assistant can help make sense of your workspace, while its AI Employee prepares proposed changes for your approval. You keep control, and your team gets a clearer view of what needs to happen next.

## Discovery questions
1. Where do tasks and follow-ups currently live?
2. How do you keep customer details and important documents organized?
3. Which recurring processes take the most time each week?
4. How do you know what is overdue or needs attention?
5. What would need to be true for a new system to be worth adopting?

## 15-minute demo
1. Ask about the prospect's current workflow (2 min).
2. Show the dashboard and priority queue (2 min).
3. Create a task and customer record (3 min).
4. Show documents, calendar and a customer email draft (3 min).
5. Demonstrate the AI Assistant and an AI Employee proposal; explicitly approve or reject it (3 min).
6. Explain workspace security, pricing and next step (2 min).

## Founding 10 offer — internal terms to finalize before advertising
The agreed concept is 20% off the customer's selected Starter or Pro subscription for the first 12 months, plus priority onboarding/support and early feature access. Effective monthly amounts are Starter $15.20 and Pro $39.20 before applicable taxes. Configure the discount in Stripe and publish clear eligibility, start/end, renewal and cancellation terms before making the offer publicly. Do not promise benefits that cannot be staffed.

## Partner program — future release
The planned partner concept is a portal for referral tracking and commissions. A 20% commission on referred subscription revenue for the first 12 months was discussed, but the portal, attribution rules, payout schedule, tax handling, fraud controls and partner agreement are not implemented. Do not accept partner referrals under an informal promise until those terms and systems exist.

## Qualification and pipeline fields
Company, website, industry, estimated size, decision-maker, verified business contact, operational pain point, relevant SILO workflow, date contacted, status, next follow-up, demo date, estimated opportunity and outcome. Research contacts from public business sources and respect applicable outreach and privacy rules.
