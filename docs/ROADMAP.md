# SILO Product Roadmap

This roadmap records the agreed long-term direction. Items below are not included in the current release unless they are explicitly listed in `docs/PRODUCT_SCOPE.md` as implemented.

## 1. Launch hardening
- Complete dependency installation and native SQLite testing on the deployment runtime.
- Complete end-to-end tests for registration, verification, workspace isolation, AI proposals, backups, billing and email.
- Configure hosted AI, Stripe, Resend, HTTPS, monitoring and reliable backups.
- Finalize legal policies and customer disclosures with qualified professional review.
- Run accessibility, browser/mobile, load and independent security reviews.

## 2. SILO Executive Director and AI workforce
- Build a central orchestration service with deterministic permission enforcement.
- Add true department directors and specialized AI Employees that coordinate through explicit, logged task handoffs.
- Add role descriptions, goals, measurable performance and department-level reporting.
- Add a browseable AI Employee Marketplace and Department Marketplace only after the underlying agents execute reliably.
- Add custom AI Employee creation with scoped tools, data access and review requirements.

## 3. Owner control and autonomy
- Manual, Assisted, Delegated and Away modes.
- Per-employee and per-department permissions, approved action categories, spending limits and expiration dates.
- Mandatory approval gates for financial, customer-facing, destructive or externally visible actions.
- Emergency stop, escalation rules, owner notifications and tamper-evident activity history.

## 4. Business intelligence and financial operations
- Business health dashboard, goals, KPIs, forecasts, risks and growth opportunities.
- Customer-workspace financial records only after the scope and accounting boundaries are defined.
- SILO operator-only financial center for subscriptions, enterprise contracts, implementation revenue, expenses, AI/provider costs, CAC, margins, MRR and cash flow.
- Monthly owner report with revenue, spending, retained cash and recommendations.

## 5. Sales, partners and customer success
- SILO-native CRM pipeline for prospects, assessments, demos, proposals, contracts and follow-ups.
- Partner portal with attribution, referral status, commissions, payout history, partner terms and fraud controls.
- Customer Success and Support workflows, onboarding progress, retention signals and renewal communications.
- Business Transformation packages and implementation project tracking.

## 6. Scale
- Multi-business portfolio dashboard with strict workspace isolation.
- Mobile experience for approvals, messages, briefings, alerts and workforce oversight.
- Enterprise organization structures, integrations, custom reporting, dedicated support and eventual white-label options.

## Architecture rule
AI may interpret, recommend and prepare actions. Authentication, authorization, money movement, permissions, rate limits, data isolation, approval enforcement and audit logging must remain deterministic application logic—not decisions delegated to a language model.
